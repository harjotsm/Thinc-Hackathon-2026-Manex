03OUR SOLUTION
One loop · end-to-end in under two minutes

Listen. Reason. Resolve. Remember.

Resolve turns every quality input — spoken, logged, scanned — into a typed signal, correlates signals into a single incident, closes the loop with a verified initiative, and writes a typed lesson that surfaces the next time a similar alarm fires.
STEP 01 · LISTEN

Every voice
a signal.

Push-to-talk on every radio. Noise-robust ASR. Every utterance becomes a typed signal with equipment, shift and confidence attached.
